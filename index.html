DEEP ROOTS

an unfolding story.

A family’s vision. A place still becoming.

Pino and Franca Passoni discovered Mormoraia in 1980. A decade later, a year after the birth of their son Alessandro, they began restoring the estate and its vineyards. They kept the old stone arches, timber beams and terracotta floors that give the house its character.In 1980, Pino and Franca Passoni found Mormoraia and began to imagine its future. Buildings were restored, vineyards renewed and a family project grew into a shared way of life. Today, Alessandro carries that vision forward, bringing hospitality and the working land ever closer together.
