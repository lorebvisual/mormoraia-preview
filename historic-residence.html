A house with a past.A room of your own.

Rooms & Suites.

When the Passoni family restored the former convent, they kept its stone arches, timber beams and terracotta floors. Today, each room and suite has a character of its own. You might fall for the intimacy of a Classic Room, or settle into a Duplex Suite and enjoy the quiet pleasure of having two floors to yourself.
